# Quick start

1. Open **Window > Extensions > D-Pad**.
<br>
2. Select one or more layers in your comp.
<br>
3. Choose a mode with the switcher at the top of the panel: **Anchor Point**, **Position** or **Attach Null**.
<br>
4. Press a direction on the pad. For example, in Anchor Point mode, the bottom-centre button moves the anchor point to the bottom-centre of each layer without moving the layer.
<br>
5. Hold **Opt/Alt** while clicking for each button's alternate action.
<br>
6. Every press is a single undo step: **Edit > Undo** puts everything back.
