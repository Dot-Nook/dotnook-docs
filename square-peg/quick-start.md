# Quick start

1. **Create a new Square Peg instance**: click the default button in the panel to generate a new Square Peg shape layer on your timeline.
2. **Adjust with the pseudo-effect**: Square Peg adds a pseudo-effect to the shape layer. Use it to control sizing, corner radii, curvature and colours.
3. **Save a preset for later**: the panel has five preset slots. Select a layer with a Square Peg effect and click an empty slot to save it. Click a filled slot to generate that preset as a new layer.
